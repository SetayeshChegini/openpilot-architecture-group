Assignment overview

Proposed Conceptual Architecture

Use software-architecture concepts from EECS 4314 to propose a suitable high-level design. Do not present the proposal as OpenPilot's verified implementation.

Updated professor clarification: The report is a proposed conceptual model, not a reverse-engineering study. Use the official comma.ai product overview only for advertised features and general purpose. Do not inspect or cite OpenPilot source code, its repository or README, internal modules, processes, classes, functions, messages, or source-code relationships. Label every architecture diagram and design statement as a Group H proposal or assumption.

1Read Requirements

Purpose: Establish the exact scope before writing.

Actions: Read the instructions and Appendix A; list mandatory sections, diagrams, formatting, deadline, publication, and submission rules.

Responsible: Setayesh coordinates; every member confirms their assigned requirements.

Tools & sources: Assignment handout, course instructions, shared outline.

Expected output: Team outline, requirement matrix, and ownership map.

Dependencies: None; this starts the report.

Complete when: Every mandatory element has an owner and planned location.

2Research

Purpose: Separate product facts from conceptual design assumptions.

Actions: Use the official comma.ai product overview only for OpenPilot's advertised features, general purpose, driver responsibility, and product-level limitations. Use EECS 4314 course materials and Group H's agreed presentation model to justify architectural choices. Record whether each statement is a sourced product fact or a Group H proposal.

Responsible: All members research their assigned topics independently.

Tools & sources: comma.ai product overview, EECS 4314 course materials, Group H presentation materials, Zotero or shared references.

Expected output: Product notes, course-based design rationale, citations, and an explicit assumptions list.

Dependencies: The requirement matrix identifies what evidence is needed.

Complete when: Every product claim has an allowed source and every architectural statement is labeled as a proposal or assumption.

3Understand Components

Purpose: Establish a common proposed model and vocabulary.

Actions: Define the major conceptual subsystems, responsibilities, information inputs and outputs, system boundaries, and supporting services using the Group H presentation names.

Responsible: Spence leads; Mehdi and David validate names needed for their analyses.

Tools & sources: EECS 4314 course materials, comma.ai product overview, Group H presentation, and shared glossary.

Expected output: Proposed subsystem inventory and agreed conceptual names.

Dependencies: Product-level research and the agreed presentation model.

Complete when: The team agrees on one proposed subsystem model and clearly states that it is not a verified implementation map.

4Analyze Interactions

Purpose: Explain how the proposed responsibilities would cooperate across boundaries.

Actions: Design conceptual data flow, control flow, concurrency, external interfaces, use cases, dependencies, and architectural decisions. State assumptions and avoid implementation-specific names or relationships.

Responsible: Mehdi leads flow/concurrency; David leads interfaces/use cases; Dan begins trade-off analysis.

Tools & sources: Agreed proposed architecture, EECS 4314 concepts, product-level use cases, and the shared glossary.

Expected output: Proposal-based interaction narratives and scenario traces.

Dependencies: Agreed conceptual model from stage 3.

Complete when: Each interaction is justified as a design choice and is not presented as actual OpenPilot behavior.

5Create Diagrams

Purpose: Make the proposed structure, dependencies, and behavior understandable.

Actions: Create the architecture overview, dependency diagram, ACC sequence diagram, and driver-monitoring sequence or state diagram; label arrows, add legends, and mark each diagram as Group H's proposed conceptual model.

Responsible: Spence owns architecture/dependency; Mehdi owns sequence 1; David owns sequence/state 2.

Tools & sources: diagrams.net or Mermaid, agreed proposed architecture notes, and EECS 4314 course concepts.

Expected output: Readable SVG or PNG diagrams plus editable sources.

Dependencies: Proposed component and interaction analysis.

Complete when: Diagrams are accessible, labeled, explained, internally consistent, and unmistakably presented as proposals.

6Write Sections

Purpose: Convert evidence and diagrams into coherent conceptual analysis.

Actions: Draft assigned sections, explain every diagram, cite sources, state assumptions, and connect each section to the whole system.

Responsible: All members write their assigned contribution.

Tools & sources: Google Docs or Word, research notes, diagrams, shared glossary.

Expected output: Editable section drafts with references and supporting artifacts.

Dependencies: Sufficient research and initial diagrams.

Complete when: Each member's package satisfies their Definition of Done and is ready for review.

7Peer Review

Purpose: Detect conceptual conflicts, unsupported claims, gaps, duplication, and readability problems.

Actions: Cross-check names, relationships, diagrams, citations, product facts, design assumptions, and repeated material. Remove any claim that presents the proposal as OpenPilot's real implementation.

Responsible: All members review another contribution; Dan and Setayesh consolidate issues.

Tools & sources: Shared drafts, comments, issue list, comma.ai product overview, course materials, and the agreed Group H model.

Expected output: Reviewed sections, resolved comments, and a remaining-issues list.

Dependencies: Individual drafts and diagrams.

Complete when: Critical conceptual issues are resolved and ownership is assigned for any remaining edits.

8Integrate Report

Purpose: Turn separate contributions into one unified report.

Actions: Combine sections, normalize headings and terminology, place diagrams, merge references, remove duplication, and check the complete narrative.

Responsible: Setayesh integrates; every member verifies their content after integration.

Tools & sources: Master document, shared glossary, reviewed artifacts, consolidated references.

Expected output: One coherent complete report draft.

Dependencies: Peer-reviewed contributions.

Complete when: Every required section is present, connected, and attributable to reliable sources.

9Format

Purpose: Meet presentation, length, accessibility, and citation requirements.

Actions: Enforce 10–15 pages, single spacing, 12-point font, consistent captions, table styles, headings, references, links, and PDF readability.

Responsible: Setayesh coordinates; all members help shorten or clarify their sections.

Tools & sources: Master document styles, PDF export, accessibility and link checks.

Expected output: Submission-ready report and final PDF candidate.

Dependencies: Integrated report.

Complete when: Formatting rules, page limit, links, citations, and diagram labels pass final review.

10Publish & Submit

Purpose: Deliver a verifiable final artifact before the deadline.

Actions: Export the final PDF, open and inspect it, publish it on the Group H website, verify the public link, and complete the required submission.

Responsible: Setayesh publishes and submits; the team confirms the correct final file.

Tools & sources: PDF viewer, Group H repository, GitHub Pages, course submission system.

Expected output: Public report URL and confirmed submission.

Dependencies: Approved formatted report.

Complete when: The exact final PDF is publicly accessible and submission is confirmed before October 25 at 11:59 PM.
